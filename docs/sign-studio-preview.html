Sign Studio

Type a product and a price or a percentage, pick the sale, print. Every sign comes out of one template, so every sign in every store looks like one family. This page is a review copy: nothing on it is wired to the app, and docs/ never reaches production. The signs, though, are real: the same drawing the build will print, laid out in print points on US Letter.

Decided on 24 Sep. US Letter. Labels: None, Sale, Flash Sale, Manager Special, Blow Out, and custom text up to 12 characters. Product names up to 32 characters, never below 0.6-inch letters. Managers (district managers included), admins, superusers. Print first; saving comes in the second release. Picked: layout C; Poppins for words and Luckiest Guy for numbers; an italic label; deep green; raised cents. Sign types: Price, % Off, and Us vs Them.
Still open.
  1. Long names on portrait. Poppins has no narrow widths, so a name over about 22 characters fits only the landscape sign at 0.6 in. Allow a third line on portrait, or cap names at 22 characters?
  2. Two prices on landscape. Each half holds about 7 capitals a line at 0.6 in, so one long word (SECTIONAL, MATTRESSES) doesn't fit, and the PRD then blocks both orientations. Let the orientation that fits print on its own?
  3. A fine-print line for an item code and date, as stores write on signs today?
  4. Optional: print any sign from here and hang it on a store shelf.

The sign design: decided

Your picks from 24 Sep, now fixed for every sign. They are template settings: managers never see them, so every store's signs match.

Typefacepicked

Poppins for words: names, labels, notes, YOU PAY, OFF. Luckiest Guy for numbers: prices, $, ¢ and %. It is the app's own Bargain Lane pair, so no new font comes in.

Sale labelpicked

Italic, in Poppins Black Italic, leaning with the wordmark. Luckiest Guy has no italic, so the numbers stay upright.

Greenpicked

Deep #20792B for the border, the label and YOU PAY: 5.5:1 on white, and it prints rich on office lasers. The logo keeps its own lighter green.

Centspicked

Raised: small cents beside big dollars ($2⁵⁰), and 99¢ under a dollar. On % Off the % is raised the same way, with OFF under it.

How the store prints it view only

A black-and-white laser turns the greens grey and the yellow dot pale. The words always carry the meaning, and the dot has a dark outline so it survives.

One system, every label

Every label choice, and the awkward cases, from the same template. Same logo, same corner, same letter size for every preset label; only the words and the price change. The long names show the fit rule working: two lines before smaller letters, and never under 0.6 in. Then the two other sign types. % Off makes the percentage the hero, with the % raised and OFF under it. On Us vs Them the discount is worked out from the two prices and rounded down, and their price prints at most 40% the size of YOU PAY, so a glance lands on what the customer pays.

What gets built

The layout and the design are picked; this is what gets built. Nothing below exists yet.

First release · frontend onlyLive when merged
  • The page as layout C: what's on sale, then which sale, then print.
  • Three sign types: Price (one or two prices), % Off (one or two percentages) and Us vs Them (their price, the worked-out discount, you pay).
  • Marketing ▸ Sign Studio for managers, admins and superusers. Its own guard in the router, equal to its sidebar gate, so the phone Menu can't show a dead row.
  • The renderer as its own module: one layout model feeds the preview, Print and the PDF, so they cannot disagree.
  • Real-text PDF: jsPDF with Poppins and Luckiest Guy embedded as static TrueType files. Logo, fonts and jsPDF self-hosted and precached, so it keeps working if the store Wi-Fi drops.
  • Its own print container and page size; the app's print rule would print a blank page.
  • The sign in progress survives an app update.
  • No worker change, no migration, nothing for you to deploy.
